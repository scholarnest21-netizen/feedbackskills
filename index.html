7.1

Objectives of Feedback

Feedback is the expression of an assessment of the level and the nature of performance in a task assigned, targeting a certain goal. The main objective of feedback is to help people improve their performance — it is a constructive help or advice whether phrased with positive or negative comments.

Level & Requirement
→
Set the Target

Methodology & Evaluation
📌 Appraisal System
Feedback is an appraisal system. The person giving feedback must know to what extent that feedback is going to improve the person receiving it.
🗣️ Phrase It Properly
Before giving feedback one should phrase it properly so that it does not turn out to be offensive. The receiver must be ready and willing to receive it.
🚀 Drive Improvement
Feedback should draw the target person's attention to areas where improvement is required and possible. It must positively influence the tenor and direction of the task. It should never be imposed.
Key Principle: It is necessary to let ourselves know how others see our performance and how it has been evaluated. Feedback should go to him or her as a positive proposal aiming at acceptability and follow-up for anticipated improvement in future.
🔍
7.2

Nature of Feedback

Feedback may be a formal or an informal appraisal process. Telling people about their performance may bring us on difficult ground — a word out of place may offend a person. Therefore, while phrasing feedback we must exercise conversation control.

When Given
📅 Timing
Feedback is given most often when it is requested. Where there is a formal system, it is given as a routine appraisal. Sometimes on exigencies it is given to effect improvement in performance. It must also state what standards it aims at.
How Phrased
📝 Based on Facts
Feedback should be based on facts and it should be phrased giving factual details. Our feedback should not generate an ill-feeling. A proper observation of performance is required for proper feedback.
⭐
7.3

Characteristics of Feedback

Effective feedback has five key characteristics — each one is essential for the feedback to serve its purpose of facilitating improvement.

👤
Personal Element
Feedback is based on one's personal evaluation — it may vary from person to person. It is related to a person and may even involve personal allegations or name-calling. However, feedback can always be converted into a positive proposal.
👁️
Observation
Feedback is based on observation of a person's performance. It should depict the factual position. Proper feedback is based on actual situation — we should comment only on what we see, not act in a prejudiced manner.
🔬
Detailed & Specific
Feedback with a detailed description of the event concentrates on facts and leads to improvement. Unless feedback is made specific about the lapses in manner, methodology, or personal trait, improvement cannot take place.
🎼
Tone
The tone of feedback is very important — it could be positive or negative, formal or informal. A sincere and critical feedback related to a specific area becomes very effective. How feedback is delivered has a lot of significance in its expected consequence.
🤝
Approach
The best kind of approach facilitates a self-critique. By asking appropriate open-ended questions we can get the other person to give the feedback themselves. The approach in feedback should be supportive and constructive.
⚡
7.4

Consequences of Feedback

The nature of feedback directly determines its consequence — whether it produces improvement or generates defensiveness.

✅ Sincere, Positive & Constructive Feedback
May effect an expected change or improvement in performance. Even when feedback does not look like advice, one should regard the criticism as advice — consider a negative statement as a positive proposal, and convert the same into a positive proposal for action. Giving personal feedback without raising any offensiveness, aggression or defensiveness is an art.
❌ Critical or General Feedback
Proves ineffective — makes the recipient not know how to respond. Sometimes someone is directly critical of us because we have not performed as per their wish. In consequence we may feel defensive or want to attack the person who gives the feedback. An attacking response may be right but may not get us any further feedback.
Best Practice: We should listen to what the other person has to say about our feedback and if he or she makes excuses and apologises, convert these positive results by taking a problem-centred attitude.
💻
7.5

Role of Modern Technology in Feedback

Many of us do not like to receive criticism of ourselves. To deal with this problem, modern technology provides us with powerful tools to see our own performance objectively.

🎙️
Audio Recorders
Learn about your tone of delivery, your voice quality, the use of non-verbal means, and mannerisms apart from proficiency in the language used.
📹
Video Recorders
See on your own how you perform in a discussion, meeting, programmed speech, or oral presentation. Review and improve your performance.
📊
Impact Analysis
Modern technology helps see the impact of your performance in terms of the response of other participants, how well you use time, and how well you express viewpoints.
🔄
Behaviour Adjustment
These means help get a clear picture of your own performance which, unlike indirect feedback, may make you adjust your behaviour accordingly and improve performance.
🛠️
7.6

Feedback Skills

Seven core skills define effective feedback delivery. Each skill ensures that the feedback achieves its purpose — improvement — without damaging the relationship.

1
👁️
Observations without Judgements
Make personal feedback descriptive with frankness, sincerity and clarity. Do not try to be evaluative. Describe observations without making judgements showing your leanings or intentions towards the facts, being good or bad. Leave the assessment to the individuals — the very description may turn out to be intelligent feedback.
2
🎯
Concentration on Specificity
Feedback should not only be factual but point-specific. It should not be a general assessment or description — it should be issue-specific. This kind of feedback becomes easier for someone to relate to rather than a response to general statements.
3
🌱
To be Positive and Constructive
Try to be constructive in feedback to have positive results. Negative statements may offend people. Do not tell people what they should not do — help them indirectly to find out what is to be done. Also tell the person the positive points of their work and working.
4
🗺️
Indicating the Ways to Improve
Explain and emphasise the practical purpose of the feedback, and say that if properly followed the receiver may benefit from it. Feedback should suggest specific areas where improvements are required and how the improvements will be under the control of the recipient.
5
🤲
Generating a Feel for Receiving Feedback
Do not impose feedback on someone — unless feedback is asked for, it should not be given. If the person does not like to receive the feedback, they should be clearly told why the feedback is given to them.
6
⏰
Proper Timing of Feedback
Feedback should be timed properly. Feedback is not saying a few quick words to someone in a corridor to improve their performance. Judge particularly when someone is in a mood to receive feedback. Taking enough time to explain the necessity of feedback is important.
7
🤝
Feedback for Agreement
Feedback should not create differences or rivals. Feedback should be so phrased that everything is clarified and there is a resultant agreement between the recipient and the giver of feedback.
📥
7.7

Receiving Feedback

We should not try to be aggressive to someone who gives us feedback. A person who is very critical about our performance at worst makes us defensive. We should be positive and sportive enough to receive our criticism. Following are guidelines for us as recipients:

1
Take criticism as advice — do not be defensive or aggressive.
2
Make a brief and accurate summary of the criticism received.
3
Show readiness and inquisitiveness to learn about the other person's feedback about us.
4
Take the feedback in a problem-solving way — instead of getting angry, smile at the mentioning of the appropriate point of criticism.
5
Seek suggestions from the person giving feedback regarding the ways and means of improvement.
6
Express gratitude to the person who has found time to find out good and bad points of our nature and working impartially and objectively.
7
Start improving in the areas and issues where improvements are required.

9.1

Variables Characterizing Style

Style is the way we select the words and arrange them as elements of various structures. Our style depends also upon our readers — we should be aware of our readers or audience and use the language that suits them depending upon the thought and occasion.

Definition: Style is thus the way we select the words and arrange them as elements of various structures. Clean writing means finding the right words for right use. Our command of words is nothing but a command over life and experience.
🔤
Choice of Words
Selecting the right, familiar, concrete, specific, and short word for maximum clarity and impact.
✂️
Discarding Modifiers
Modifiers of nouns and verbs make text vague and subjective — use them carefully to avoid confusion.
🚫
Discarding Fancy Words
High-flying, ornamental, bombastic words down the meaning. Plain words that are more effective should be selected.
🔁
Discarding Clichés
Words that have lost their effectiveness due to overuse can never be appropriate for effective style.
🌫️
Discarding Woolly Thinking Words
Words like "numberwise" and "resolution-wise" impede communication and should be discarded.
🌍
Discarding Foreign Words
Foreign words impress readers but mystify them very soon. Accepted foreign abbreviations need not be avoided.
🔄
Controlling Passive Voice
Excessive passive voice makes style stodgy. Active voice conveys businesslike commitment and urgency. Balance is required.
👤
Right Personal Pronoun
Modern trend is to use the first person pronoun directly. Conservative organizations continue using the third person.
🗑️
Avoiding Redundancy
Eliminate parts of a message whose removal does not yield a loss of information. Repetitions and unnecessary words are superfluous.
📏
Sentence Structure & Length
Deciding placement of modifiers, limiting complex sentences, and right use of grammar for readability.
📄
Paragraph Structure & Length
Each paragraph conveys one idea through four types of sentences: introducers, developers, modulators, terminators.
🌟
Other Important Features
Signposts, no surrender to words, rewriting, affecting desirable tone and colour in the text.
🔤
9.1.1

Choice of Words

For effecting a style in writing, the choice of right words and phrases is very important. There are different words for different contexts — the suitability of words depends upon the suitability of their meanings.

✅
The Right Word
For attaining an effective style there is a need for a constant concern for selecting the right word at the right place and a desire to extract the meaning.
🤝
The Familiar Word
Familiar words convey sharp images; unfamiliar ones convey blurred ones. Avoid excessive jargon (technical terminology) as it causes trouble for readers and displays verbosity.
🧱
The Concrete Word
Concrete words have a limited range of interpretation and convey more definite meanings than abstract words. They are more forceful — prefer them for effective style.
🎯
The Specific Word
Unlike general words, specific words effect clarity in writing. Specific words create the image immediately in the mind of the reader.
✂️
The Short Word
For effective style the number of longer words (3+ syllables) should be limited. Short words (1–2 syllables) are simple and direct — using more long words than short ones overtaxes the reader.

Words & Patterns to Discard

For effective style, avoid these categories of words:

✗
Modifiers that make text vague and subjective
✗
Fancy, ornamental or bombastic words
✗
Clichés — words overused to the point of losing effectiveness
✗
Words denoting woolly thinking (e.g. "numberwise")
✗
Foreign words (except accepted abbreviations)
✗
Excessive passive voice constructions
✗
Redundant and superfluous repetitions
✗
Unfamiliar jargon and complicated terminology
📏
9.1.10

Sentence Structure and Length

The effective style yields a greater comprehension in the text. Too many complex and compound sentences hamper reading comprehension. A balance and harmony between simple, complex, and compound sentences is needed. Variety in length avoids monotony.

📌
Placement of Modifiers
Experiment with placement of adjectives and adverbs. Handling of adverbs requires greater caution — the change in their placement changes the meaning too.
⚖️
Balance Complex & Compound
Too many long sentences increase difficulty; too many short sentences in a series read like shots at a rifle range. There should be a short sentence in the midst of several long sentences for variety.
📖
Right Use of Grammar
Don't shorten a sentence with a conjunction and don't split infinitives. There must be a verb in every sentence. However, vivid impacts are created when these rules are broken — this has a powerful impact on the reader.
Pro Tip: Sentences should begin with a verb or a phrase occasionally. We should find out by reading aloud whether our sentences are too long or too short.
📄
9.1.11

Paragraph Structure and Length

A sentence conveys one thought whereas a paragraph conveys one idea. The idea is expressed through a topic sentence — which may be placed at the beginning or simply implied. Sentences in a paragraph are of four types:

Introducers
Establish the topic and provide smooth transition from the preceding paragraph
Developers
Develop the idea, clarify it with details and thoughts
Modulators
Provide smooth transition between the sentences of the paragraph
Terminators
Conclude the discussion by summarizing the contents of the paragraph
📐 Ideal Text Length
The ideal text consists of four to six paragraphs of two to ten sentences each. A skilful writer breaks the text into short paragraphs, gives due emphasis to the topic sentence and checks transitional devices for smooth movement and development.
🔗 Logical Arrangement
Long paragraphs do not provide ease in reading. A paragraph dealing with a complex idea naturally increases its length. Attention is paid to the logical arrangement of all sentences, ensuring all contribute to the development of the idea.
🌟
9.2

Other Important Features of Effective Style

Beyond word choice and sentence structure, effective style has four additional hallmarks that distinguish skilful writers.

🪧
Signposts
An efficient writer provides signposts in the text to tell the reader what is taken up at various stages or sections. This helps the reader follow the line of thought very easily. The signpost comes in the form of a sentence or a short paragraph between two paragraphs.
🧠
No Surrender to Words
A skilful writer does not surrender to words. In the process of writing, the words that encode facts or information also try to take the writer's mind into their grip. The author so trapped sacrifices one idea for the sake of another — a skilful writer sticks to their line of thinking.
✏️
Rewriting
A skilful writer keeps on rewriting the text by correcting errors in punctuations, spellings, capitalization, following stylistic conventions the text deserves, and deleting or rearranging material to effect greater clarity and coherence. They experiment with the order of the text to effect greater readability.
🎨
Affecting Desirable Tone and Colour
A skilful writer tries to effect the desirable tone and colour in the text — a style that generates and sustains the reader's interest. They take the help of various means creating suspense, surprise, anxiety, and inquisitiveness by a technique of orchestration, providing magnetic thoughts from various corners so that the reader is glued to the text.
Clean Writing = Finding the right words for right use. There are different registers for different fields — there should not be a mixture of traditional standard variety and modern colloquial variety of English, as this makes words and expressions out of place.
